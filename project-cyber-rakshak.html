✦ About

About Project Cyber Rakshak

Building a cyber-aware nation through education and awareness

Why Project Cyber Rakshak?

India is rapidly becoming one of the world's largest digital economies. From online banking and UPI payments to social media, online shopping, digital classrooms, and remote work, technology has become a part of our everyday lives.

Unfortunately, cyber criminals are evolving just as quickly. Every day, thousands of people lose their savings, personal information, and digital identity due to scams such as fake job offers, phishing attacks, QR code frauds, OTP scams, investment frauds, AI voice cloning, fake customer care numbers, and social media hacking.

🛡️ Many of these incidents happen not because people are careless, but because they are unaware of how these scams work.

Project Cyber Rakshak was created to bridge this awareness gap. Our mission is to educate, empower, and protect every citizen by making cyber safety simple, practical, and accessible to everyone.

✦ Who We Work With

Who We Work With

Empowering every section of society with cyber safety knowledge

👨‍🎓 Students

Interactive sessions on cyber safety, gaming risks, cyber bullying, phishing attacks, digital etiquette, and responsible social media usage.

👩 Women

Awareness on online harassment, identity theft, social media privacy, financial scams, and digital self-protection.

👴 Senior Citizens

Helping senior citizens identify fake banking calls, OTP frauds, QR code scams, and emotional manipulation by cyber criminals.

👨‍💼 Working Professionals

Corporate cyber awareness covering email security, phishing, password hygiene, data protection, and workplace cyber threats.

🏫 Schools & Colleges

Interactive seminars, workshops, awareness drives, competitions, and practical demonstrations.

🏢 Corporate Organizations

Customized cyber awareness programs for employees to build safer digital workplaces.
