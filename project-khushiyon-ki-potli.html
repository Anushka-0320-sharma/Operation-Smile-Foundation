✦ Why We Started

Why Khushiyon Ki Potli

Giving every child a reason to smile

For many of us, birthdays mean cakes, gifts, decorations, photographs, and celebrations with family and friends.

But for countless children living in underserved communities, birthdays often pass like any other ordinary day.

❌ No cake.
❌ No candles.
❌ No gifts.
❌ No celebration.

Not because they don't deserve happiness, but because life has demanded that basic needs come before celebrations.

We started Project Khushiyon Ki Potli to change that reality.

🎯 Our mission is simple: To encourage people to celebrate their birthdays by sharing happiness with children who deserve to feel special too.

🎯

Our Mission

To inspire individuals, families, students, professionals, and communities to celebrate birthdays with compassion by creating joyful experiences for children who rarely get the opportunity to celebrate their own special day.

We believe that a birthday becomes truly meaningful when it creates happiness beyond ourselves.

✦ What We Do

Creating Smiles, One Birthday at a Time

Turning ordinary birthdays into extraordinary moments of joy

🎂

Birthday Celebrations

We organize joyful birthday celebrations where volunteers and birthday hosts spend quality time with children through cake cutting, games, music, laughter, and heartfelt interactions.

🎈
🎁

Gifts That Create Smiles

Every celebration includes thoughtfully selected gifts such as stationery, toys, books, chocolates, school supplies, or daily essentials depending on the needs of the children.

✨
❤️

Creating Memories

More than distributing gifts, we focus on creating emotional experiences that remind every child that they are loved, valued, and celebrated.

💝
🌈

Inspiring Society

Our initiative encourages people to rethink birthday celebrations and transform one personal occasion into an opportunity to spread kindness and inspire others.

🌟
✦ How It Works

Your Birthday, Their Smile

Four simple steps to create a birthday that matters

1

Tell Us Your Birthday

Choose your preferred celebration date and connect with our team.

2

We Plan Everything Together

We coordinate with schools, shelter homes, communities, or NGOs and help organize the celebration.

3

Celebrate With Children

Cut the cake, distribute gifts, spend time together, play games, click photographs, and create unforgettable memories.

4

Share Your Smile

Leave with a heart full of gratitude, knowing your birthday made a meaningful difference.
